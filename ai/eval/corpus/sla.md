# Response time targets

Every ticket has a first-response target based on its priority. Urgent tickets have a first-response target of 1 hour. High priority tickets have 4 hours. Normal priority tickets have 1 business day. Low priority tickets have 3 business days.

Targets are measured in business hours, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 in the workspace time zone. The clock pauses while a ticket is in pending status, waiting for the customer.
