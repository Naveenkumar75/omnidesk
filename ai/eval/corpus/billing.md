# Billing and invoices

Invoices are generated on the first day of each month and sent to the billing contact. You can download past invoices as PDF files from Settings, then Billing.

To change your payment method, go to Settings, then Billing, and select "Update card". Changes apply from the next billing cycle.

Refunds are available within 14 days of a charge for annual plans. Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months.
